Our Story

Why We Started The Hungry Critic

📖

The Hungry Critic began as a passion project focused on documenting unforgettable food experiences across Indian cities.

Instead of paid rankings or promotional lists, we wanted to build a publication rooted in genuine editorial taste and independent criticism.

Today, we continue to explore restaurants, cafés, chefs and street food cultures through thoughtful storytelling and immersive design.
